Keep the personal preference

Lead with the ask or answer. Stop when it is clear. Use plain language and only necessary identifiers. Informal pings omit the final period by default; questions keep their question marks.

Make the scope portable

Personal messages need no repository. Supplied PR facts can produce a reply without repeating an audit. A draft stays a draft unless sending is authorized, with the exact recipient and thread.
