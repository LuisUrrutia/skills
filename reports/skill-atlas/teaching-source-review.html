Teach ideas and design learning progressions

teach owns lessons, examples, optional practice, and feedback. learning-plan owns sequence, workload, courses, and progress adjustments. explain-code and explain-decisions supply evidence for code mechanisms and historical reasons.

Selected mechanisms

pstack supplies learner-aware explanations; Matt Pocock connects goals to practice and evidence. Cursor teaching supplies progression and retrospectives. HumanLayer and Matthew Blode help choose and check useful representations. Compound Engineering supplies intake and application-oriented checks.

Conflicting donor rules are resolved by the learner's request: quizzes, files, HTML, and multi-session courses are optional. A request for a complete course still requires the requested lesson content. Unknown company economics remain unknown, and another user's saved profile does not define this learner.
