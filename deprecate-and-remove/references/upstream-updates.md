with this skill as the target. If unavailable, report the missing dependency and

edits. "Update deprecate-and-remove from upstream" authorizes compatible changes
and validation. Neither creates a schedule. Read `../origin.txt` for revisions,
review cursors, borrowed ideas, and local choices.

consumer responsibility, verified access closure, retained data and contracts,
and completion at the actual observed stage. Keep delivery, investigation,
design, documentation, and verification with their existing owners.

observation windows, deletion of still-needed tests or records, a fixed toolchain,
or authority to send notices or mutate live resources beyond the active task.

updates. Ordinary retirement does not fetch or install these source collections.
