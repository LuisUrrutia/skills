# Deliverable Templates

Use these templates when creating startup analysis outputs.

## Single-File Quick Verdict

```markdown
# [Idea Name] Quick Verdict

## Snapshot
- Decision: [GO | CONDITIONAL GO | PIVOT | NO-GO]
- Score: [0-100]
- Confidence: [high | medium | low]

## What is promising
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]

## What is risky
- [Risk 1]
- [Risk 2]

## Must-test assumptions
1. [Assumption + test]
2. [Assumption + test]
3. [Assumption + test]

## 14-day action plan
- Day 1-3: [Action]
- Day 4-7: [Action]
- Day 8-14: [Action]
```

## Multi-File Case Pack Structure

Use this exact structure and sequence:

1. `00-executive-brief.md`
2. `01-problem-customer-fit.md`
3. `02-market-opportunity.md`
4. `03-competitive-landscape.md`
5. `04-business-model-unit-economics.md`
6. `05-go-to-market-plan.md`
7. `06-financial-scenarios.md`
8. `07-validation-experiments.md`
9. `08-risk-register.md`
10. `09-90-day-execution-plan.md`
11. `assumptions-log.md`
12. `sources-and-evidence.md`

## Required Sections by File

### `00-executive-brief.md`
- Decision, score, confidence
- 3 reasons behind recommendation
- Top risks
- Immediate next actions

### `01-problem-customer-fit.md`
- Problem statement
- Target customer and trigger moment
- Current workaround
- Pain evidence and assumption list

### `02-market-opportunity.md`
- TAM/SAM/SOM with method
- Source-backed market growth trends
- Confidence and data limitations

### `03-competitive-landscape.md`
- Direct and indirect competitors
- Matrix on core buying factors
- Wedge statement and defensibility

### `04-business-model-unit-economics.md`
- Pricing and packaging hypotheses
- Unit economics baseline
- Failure points and model revisions

### `05-go-to-market-plan.md`
- Beachhead segment
- Channel strategy with expected conversion
- 90-day KPI targets

### `06-financial-scenarios.md`
- Conservative/base/upside assumptions
- Revenue/burn/runway summary
- Scenario break conditions

### `07-validation-experiments.md`
- Ranked experiment backlog
- Success thresholds and time/cost bounds
- 14-day experiment sprint

### `08-risk-register.md`
- Probability/impact matrix
- Early warning signals
- Mitigation owner and plan

### `09-90-day-execution-plan.md`
- Month-by-month execution plan
- Checkpoint criteria for continue/pivot/stop

### `assumptions-log.md`
- Assumption, status, supporting evidence, last updated

### `sources-and-evidence.md`
- Claim-to-source mapping
- Source quality rating (A/B/C)

## Quality Bar

Every deliverable should be:
- Specific (numbers over vague claims)
- Auditable (clear sources and assumptions)
- Actionable (clear next decisions and owners)
- Honest about uncertainty (confidence levels visible)
